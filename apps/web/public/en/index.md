# Know how much money you lose, and why.

Post-purchase revenue intelligence for businesses that sell on marketplaces

Rovaq AI measures observed refunds and the revenue exposed to each problem, with the evidence in plain view and every figure labeled Observed, Estimated or Inferred.

Always gross revenue, never margin. Exposure is an inferred figure, not a loss.

## Every problem, with its exposed revenue and its evidence.

A repeated theme is not the same as a theme that costs. Rovaq puts beside each problem its exposed revenue, where it comes from, the date it reaches and what is still unknown.

- Gross revenue, never margin.
- What is unknown says No data, never zero.
- Currencies kept apart, never converted.
- A person decides every action.

## The information that explains your revenue leaks is scattered.

And nobody connects it.

- Three places, no single view. Conversations sit in a support tool, orders and refunds in each marketplace, and reports in spreadsheets.
- A silent leak. Refunds that could be claimed and are not make a silent leak. Without seeing them by channel and by product, there is nowhere to act.
- A deadline that passes. Missed response times show up after the customer has already complained. Knowing when threads arrive makes it possible to staff in time.
- Figures that read as facts. Spreadsheet reports often mix currencies, treat missing data as zero and present estimates as facts.

## Every figure says where it comes from.

## Four classes of figure and a single rule.

- Observed. Recorded at the source, such as orders and settled refunds.
- Estimated. Calculated with a model or with assumptions you define. It is not a recorded fact.
- Inferred. Linked by evidence, such as the revenue of orders tied to a problem. It is not a loss and is not added across problems.
- No data. There are no eligible records. It is never shown as zero.

The rule. Under every important figure goes its seal: class, source, cut-off date and age. If the cut-off date is unknown, the seal says so.

## Four modules, and a person who decides.

Each module does one part of the work with your data. They are not autonomous agents: they propose and prepare, and a person approves every action.

- Import with provenance. Bring in HubSpot conversations and orders or refunds from Excel and CSV. Every record keeps its source and cut-off date. Marketplaces come in by export, with help from our team.
- Group into problems. Each problem shows its volume and evidence when links are confirmed. Emails and phone numbers are masked. See what repeats. Grouping with AI models is enabled per organization.
- Quantify in gross revenue. Each problem shows exposed gross revenue, labeled Inferred and never as a loss. Every figure carries class, source and cut-off. Exposure is calculated only with the problem-to-order links that an owner confirmed.
- Decide and measure. Each recommendation brings its evidence. A person decides: Proceed, Adjust or Dismiss. Nothing is sent automatically; the outcome is recorded. Proceed creates the intervention and drafts that a person reviews and sends.

## Six screens, each built to answer one question.

Walk through the product with example data. No data is from a customer.

- [Today](https://rovaq.ai/en/product/today). What needs attention today. Four key figures based on the sources you have loaded: gross revenue, refunds, stuck money and replies over 24 hours, each with period, formula and coverage.
- Refund leakage. Where returned money concentrates. See where returned money concentrates by channel and product, and download an evidence package. You review it and you file it.
- Problems with evidence. Which problem to prioritize and with which cases. Each problem is presented with its volume and its evidence when links are confirmed. Economic exposure is an Inferred figure: revenue from orders linked to the problem, not a loss.
- [Ask your data](https://rovaq.ai/en/product/ask-your-data). How this figure was reached. Type a question and get figures, tables and charts. It runs on rules, without a language model, shows how it calculated and says when it does not have the data.
- [To decide](https://rovaq.ai/en/product/to-decide). What is proposed and who decides. Every proposal arrives with its evidence and its reason. A person decides: Proceed, Adjust or Dismiss, and the outcome is recorded.
- [Data trust](https://rovaq.ai/en/product/data-trust). How much to trust a figure. A traffic light per source, the freshness of each figure, its coverage and a list of what is still unknown. It is trust in the data, not a certification.

## Each role sees what belongs to it.

- Owner. Configures, decides and approves. Manages team, notices and retention. All screens, the team, notices and retention.
- Analyst. Investigates, prepares and proposes. Sees money and imports files. Money, problems, pain points and imports. Proposes and does not approve.
- Operator. Carries out the actions assigned to them. Sees problems and actions, without money figures. Problems, assigned actions and evidence. No money figures.
- Viewer. Reads with read-only access, with no risk of changing anything. The screens that apply to them, unable to modify data, connections or decisions.

Finance and customer experience read the same problem: the money that is returned and the reason it is returned.

Authorization is decided on the server. Hiding a menu item does not protect a screen.

## Security described by what exists today and what does not yet.

What exists.

- Each organization is kept apart from the others by rules in the database, not only by the application.
- Four roles with least-privilege permissions. The platform administration permission is separate and does not grant read access to the business data of other organizations.
- Sign-in is by a link sent to your email, with email confirmation, and sessions use protected cookies.
- Connections to your sources request read permissions.
- Pain-point and claim exports are generated without personal data.
- Administration operations, team changes and retention deletions are recorded.
- When AI is involved, it classifies and drafts. It does not produce amounts, and a person decides.

What does not exist yet.

- We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification, and we do not claim GDPR compliance.
- No external penetration test has been performed.
- Two-step verification for owners is on our roadmap. It is not required today.
- We do not offer single sign-on or a customer-facing audit view.
- There are daily provider backups, without point-in-time recovery, and we make no availability commitments.

## What Rovaq does not do.

The limits are part of the product too.

- It does not calculate margin or profit. Everything is gross revenue.
- It does not convert currencies or add them. USD, CAD and MXN are kept apart.
- It does not present exposure as a loss. It is an inferred figure and is not added across problems.
- It is not an autonomous agent. It prepares the package and the draft; a person files the claim and decides every action.
- It does not promise recovery, savings or return. What is recoverable is an estimate based on assumptions you enter.
- It does not work in real time. Sync is scheduled and every screen shows its cut-off date.
- It does not connect directly with Amazon, Walmart or Shopify. Today their exports are loaded.
- It does not use a language model to answer in Ask your data and it does not search the internet.
- It holds no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification, does not claim GDPR compliance, and two-step verification for owners is on the roadmap.
- It does not replace your support tool. It reads HubSpot conversations in read-only mode and does not write to your CRM.

If anything on this list changes, it will be stated here.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Rovaq AI?

It is revenue intelligence for businesses that sell on marketplaces and support customers across channels. It connects conversations, orders and refunds, and shows every figure with its class, source and cut-off date.

### Which sources does it work with today?

With HubSpot, read-only, and with Excel and CSV files. Amazon, Walmart and Shopify are on the roadmap: today their exports are loaded with help from our team.

### Is the data updated in real time?

No. HubSpot syncs on a schedule every 5 minutes and file uploads are manual. Every figure shows the date its data reaches and how long ago it was updated.

### Does Rovaq calculate my margin or how much money I lose?

It does not calculate margin: everything is gross revenue. Refunds are observed figures. Exposure per problem is an inferred figure: the revenue of orders linked to that problem, which is not the same as a loss.

### Does Rovaq use AI and where?

Ask your data answers with rules and read-only tools, without a language model, and shows its steps. Grouping and classifying conversations can use AI models when the organization enables it. AI does not create amounts and a person decides every action.

### Does Rovaq claim refunds for me?

No. It assembles a package of aggregated evidence and a draft message. You review them and file the claim. Nothing is sent on its own and no recovered amount is promised.

### How secure is it and what certifications do you hold?

There is isolation per organization in the database, four roles, read connections and a record of administration operations. We hold no SOC 2 or ISO 27001, we have not run an external penetration test and two-step verification for owners is on the roadmap. The detail is on the Security page.

### Does Rovaq replace my support tool?

No. It reads HubSpot conversations in read-only mode and connects them with the orders and refunds you load. It does not write to your CRM or reply to your customers.
