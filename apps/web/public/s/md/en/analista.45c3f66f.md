# Analyst: investigate, prepare and propose

Sees money and imports files. Proposes and does not approve.

## What they investigate

- Refund leakage, Problems, Pain points, Finance and the money figures in Today.
- Any figure, down to its source, with the How it was calculated panel.

## What they prepare

- Imports Excel and CSV files with preview and validation.
- Builds the evidence package and exports reports with the same figures as the screen.

## Where the role ends

The analyst proposes recommendations but does not approve them or carry out interventions, and does not configure connections, retention or team. Those decisions belong to the owner.

_Example data. Not from a customer._
