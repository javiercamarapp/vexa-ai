- [Your marketplace exports are already data: how to load them with provenance](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/marketplace-exports-are-data). Published 2026-11-03. 5 min read. The files you download from your sales channels are not a pending chore. They are the history you already have, and it can be loaded with its origin in plain view.
- [Pain points: from free text in conversations to a priority backed by evidence](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/pain-points-from-text-to-priority). Published 2026-11-03. 5 min read. Counting mentions is a start. A priority also needs a size, evidence that can be opened and a list of what is not known.

When a proposal brings its evidence, a person who decides and a record of what happened, it stops being an opinion and becomes a habit.
