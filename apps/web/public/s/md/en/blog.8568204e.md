# Rovaq AI blog

Notes on refunds, the provenance of figures, pain points and decisions backed by evidence.

## Recent articles

- [Refunds are not a fixed cost: they are a leak you can locate](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/refund-leak-you-can-locate). Published 2026-10-31. 5 min read. A single total does not say what to tackle first. By channel, by product and by month, returned money starts to take shape.
- [Where every number comes from: provenance is a requirement](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/where-every-number-comes-from). Published 2026-10-31. 6 min read. A figure without an origin can only be accepted or rejected. With its source, its cut-off date and its gaps in plain view, it can be debated.
- [AI classifies and drafts; people decide](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/ai-classifies-people-decide). Published 2026-10-31. 6 min read. Where a language model belongs in a financial product, where it does not, and why the figures should never come from it.
- [One role, one view: why each role sees only what belongs to it](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/one-role-one-view). Published 2026-10-31. 5 min read. A tool with everything for everyone looks generous and delivers little. Showing less protects information and helps people work.
- [Your marketplace exports are already data: load them with provenance](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/marketplace-exports-are-data). Published 2026-10-31. 5 min read. The files you download from your sales channels are not a pending chore. They are the history you already have, and it can be loaded with its origin in plain view.
- [Pain points: from free text to a priority backed by evidence](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/pain-points-from-text-to-priority). Published 2026-10-31. 5 min read. Counting mentions is a start. A priority also needs a size, evidence that can be opened and a list of what is not known.
- [A recommendation must be debatable: evidence and outcome](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/a-recommendation-must-be-debatable). Published 2026-10-31. 5 min read. When a proposal brings its evidence, a person who decides and a record of what happened, it stops being an opinion and becomes a habit.
