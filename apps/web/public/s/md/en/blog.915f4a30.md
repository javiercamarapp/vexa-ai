# Rovaq AI blog

Notes on refunds, the provenance of figures, pain points and decisions backed by evidence.

## Recent articles

- [Refunds are not a fixed cost: they are a leak you can locate](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/refund-leak-you-can-locate). Published 2026-11-03. 6 min read. A refund total mixes things that are not alike. This article explains how to split returned money by channel, product and month, how to read a Pareto without asking more of it than it gives, and what an evidence package should hold so a claim can be reviewed.
- [Where every number comes from: provenance as a requirement, not decoration](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/where-every-number-comes-from). Published 2026-11-03. 6 min read. A dashboard that does not explain where each figure comes from forces you to believe it. This article describes what should accompany a number so it can be debated: its class, its source, its cut-off date and what is still unknown, and why that is not the same as a certification.
- [AI classifies and drafts; people decide](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/ai-classifies-people-decide). Published 2026-11-03. 5 min read. A tool that invents amounts loses trust at the first report. This article explains the principle we used to design Rovaq AI: AI helps classify and draft, figures come from verifiable records and a person decides every action.
- [One role, one view: why each role sees only what belongs to it](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/one-role-one-view). Published 2026-11-03. 5 min read. A tool that shows everything to everyone is harder to use and harder to protect. This article explains how views are split among owner, analyst, operator and viewer in Rovaq AI, why minimal permissions help clarity and what it means for authorization to be decided on the server.
- [Your marketplace exports are already data: how to load them with provenance](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/marketplace-exports-are-data). Published 2026-11-03. 5 min read. Many teams wait for a direct connector before they start analyzing their sales and refunds, without noticing that the history is already in their Excel and CSV exports. This article explains what can be loaded today in Rovaq AI, how the preview and per-row provenance work, and what remains on the roadmap.
- [Pain points: from free text in conversations to a priority backed by evidence](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/pain-points-from-text-to-priority). Published 2026-11-03. 6 min read. A theme that repeats in support conversations is a clue, not a priority. This article explains why counting mentions misleads, how to measure a reason in volume and in money without mixing the two units, why missing data is not zero and how to share the evidence without personal data.
- [What we claim and what we do not claim about security](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/what-we-claim-about-security). Published 2026-11-03. 5 min read. A useful security page separates facts from adjectives and states what is missing. This article explains which security practices we describe and anyone can point to in the product, which gaps we declare, why publishing them serves a buyer better than a promise of total security and which questions to ask any vendor.
- [A recommendation must be debatable: evidence, approval and outcome](https://rovaq.ai/en/blog/a-recommendation-must-be-debatable). Published 2026-11-03. 5 min read. A recommendation without evidence is either ignored or obeyed blindly. This article explains what a proposal must carry in order to be debated, why a small decision vocabulary and a person who approves are worth having, and how to record the outcome by measuring before and after using observed data only.

## Topics

- Refunds: where returned money concentrates and what evidence it takes to file a claim.
- Provenance: the source, the cut-off date and what is still unknown about each figure.
- Pain points: how to go from the free text of conversations to a priority with size and evidence.
- Decisions: proposals with evidence, a person who decides and an outcome that gets recorded.
