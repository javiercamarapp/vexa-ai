# Data trust: freshness, coverage and what is unknown

How much to trust a figure.

## Freshness and coverage

- A traffic light per source: Up to date, Manual load, Delayed, Alert, Paused or No data.
- The freshness of each figure: when it was recalculated and the date its data reaches.
- Coverage, with windows per channel.
- A How it was calculated panel on each figure, with its origin, period and formula.

## What is still unknown

What we do not know is a task list: the affected figure, what closes it and its basis. A source that never ran appears as No data and not as Up to date. Manual loads do not raise an alert.

## Data trust is not certification

This screen is about the quality of the figures. It is not a security or compliance certification.
