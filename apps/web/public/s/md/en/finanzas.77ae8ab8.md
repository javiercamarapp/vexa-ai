# Finance: a report by currency, bases never mixed

What the report says by currency.

## Gross revenue by currency

- Revenue, orders, refunds and refund rate by currency, with a 12-month series and the mix by channel.
- The monthly comparison only between complete months; incomplete months are dotted.
- A three-month projection with a range, labeled Estimated, which is not shown with fewer than four complete months.

## Cutoffs and reconciliation

Every figure states its currency, basis and period. What is missing appears as a task list and not as a zero. A figure with incomplete reconciliation is flagged as partial. The report is exported as CSV and PDF with the same figures as the screen.

## What it does not calculate

- It does not calculate margin or profit: everything is gross revenue.
- It does not add or convert USD, CAD and MXN, and does not subtract refunds from revenue because they are different bases.
- Exposure per problem shows as Not available until each problem is linked to its orders.

## Who sees it

Workspace administrator and analyst. The operator and read-only roles get a notice that their role has no access to this panel.
