# Today: four figures with period, formula and coverage

What needs attention today, with every figure labeled by its class and its source.

## What the screen shows

- Four key figures: gross revenue, refunds, stuck money and replies over 24 hours, each with its period.
- A Needs your attention block with alerts, such as a leak on one product, overdue requests or threads without a reply.
- The Suggested next action and a button to export the report.

## How to read a partial figure

Stuck money comes from an Excel Tracker that is loaded by hand and carries no cut-off date. Replies over 24 hours come from HubSpot. Every figure states its coverage and what is still unknown.

## What it does not calculate

- It does not calculate margin or profit: revenue is gross.
- It does not add or convert different currencies, because there is no approved exchange rate.
- An empty state says there is no data yet and never shows it as zero.

## Who sees it

All four roles, with different views. Owner and analyst see the four figures. Operator and viewer see a view of their own, without money figures.

_Example data. Not from a customer._
