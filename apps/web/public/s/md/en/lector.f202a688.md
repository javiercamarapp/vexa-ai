# Read-only: read without any risk of changing anything

Reads with read-only access.

## What they can read

- The screens that apply to them: Today, Problems with their evidence, Data trust and the actions.
- Can export downloads of what they see.

## What they cannot do

Cannot modify data, connections or decisions, and does not see money figures. Authorization is decided on the server: hiding a menu item does not protect a screen.

## Who it is for

Leadership, finance and teams that need to read the figures with their evidence without touching anything.

_Example data. Not from a customer._
