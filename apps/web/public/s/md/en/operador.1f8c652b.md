# Operator: carries out the actions assigned to you

Sees problems and actions, without money figures.

## What they receive

- The interventions assigned to them, with their stages and the drafts ready to review.
- The recommendations to decide and the open problems, ordered by number of conversations.

## What they see and do not see

Sees problems, assigned actions and evidence. Does not see money figures and cannot import files. In Ask your data it receives totals and not specific customers.

## How they record the result

Moves the intervention stages, reviews and sends the drafts, and records the outcome. Proposing is also within the role; approving is not.

_Example data. Not from a customer._
