# To decide: proposal, a person decides, outcome recorded

What is proposed and who decides.

## The life of a proposal

Each recommendation brings its evidence, why it is suggested and what is expected. A person decides, and the outcome is recorded with its before and after measurement, using observed data only.

## Proceed, Adjust or Dismiss

- Proceed creates the intervention and the drafts that a person reviews and sends. Nothing is sent automatically.
- Adjust and Dismiss ask for a reason, which is recorded. Reopening a dismissed proposal creates a new event and deletes nothing.
- Propose: owner, analyst and operator. Approve: owner only. Carry out: owner and operator.

## Outcome record

In progress shows the owner, the commitment, the stages and the drafts. Outcomes measures before and after and never presents an assumed saving.
