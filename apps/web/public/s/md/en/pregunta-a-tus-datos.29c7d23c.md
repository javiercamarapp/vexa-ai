# Ask your data: calculated figures, visible steps

How this figure was reached, in plain view.

## How an answer is calculated

You type a question and get figures, tables and charts, each with its class and coverage. Figures come from read-only tools and are calculated with rules, without a language model.

## Steps in view

- Each answer can be opened in How I got here, with the steps, the parameters, the method and the source.
- The steps were recorded by the system; they are not the internal reasoning of a model.
- You can copy, download as CSV, rate the answer and attach a file of up to 1 MB that is analyzed only in your browser.

## What it does not do

- This describes the general agent of this screen. The Finance mode has its own engine and we do not claim it is rules only.
- It does not use a language model to answer and does not search the internet.
- When it does not have the data, it says so and does not invent it. If the question does not match an available query, it asks you to clarify.
- The monthly budget limits queries; when it runs out, direct queries remain.

## Who sees it

All four roles. The operator receives totals and not specific customers.
