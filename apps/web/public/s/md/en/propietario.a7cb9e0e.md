# Owner: configure, decide and approve

Manages team, notices and retention, and approves actions. Approving belongs to this role alone.

## What they configure

- Invites the team by email with a role; invitations expire and give no access until accepted.
- Connects sources with help from our team and authorizes imports.
- Sets the organization notice delivery and the retention and deletion policy, which first shows the exact scope and deletes nothing until confirmed.

## What they decide

- Approves, adjusts or dismisses the proposals in To decide.
- Confirms the problem-to-order links that exposure depends on.
- Publishes the organization order and refund cutoffs.

## What is recorded

Administration operations, team changes and retention deletions are recorded. The platform administration permission is separate and does not grant read access to the business data of other organizations.
