# Pain points: which reason repeats and what it carries

Tell what repeats from what costs.

## Bubble map

- Each bubble is a pain point. Its size is returned money in gross dollars or volume, your choice.
- The eight largest appear in the chart and in an accessible table, with a topic filter.
- Each row opens its evidence down to the source row.

## Export without personal data

Export the evidence as CSV per pain point, without personal data. What is not known says No data and never zero.

## What it does not do

- A repeated theme is not a theme that costs: the screen puts the money it carries beside it, when it exists.
- If a source did not respond, those points show as Not available now and not as zero.

_Example data. Not from a customer._
