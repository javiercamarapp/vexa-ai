Your
AI native customer intelligence platform— secure, compliant, and ready to scale
org-wide
Dovetail is built for organizations where security, privacy, and control aren’t optional. SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance are table stakes—but what sets Dovetail apart is how it lets your team move fast without trading away governance.
Built for trust at scale
SSO, automated provisioning, and custom data retention give IT and security full control—without slowing teams down.
Drive consistency
Global fields, tags, templates, and workspace governance mean every team works from the same source of truth.
Privacy without friction
Role-based permissions keep sensitive data protected while ensuring the right people always have the context they need.
Automatic PII redaction
Names, faces, and voices are automatically redacted across text, audio, and video—compliance built in, not bolted on.
AI you can trust
Every AI-generated insight links back to its source. No black-box answers—your team can interrogate any finding with confidence.
Private AI
Dovetail runs Gen AI on AWS Bedrock in a secure, private environment. Your data never trains models and never leaves your control.
