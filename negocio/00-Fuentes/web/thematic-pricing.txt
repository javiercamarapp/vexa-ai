Common questions
Have questions? We have answers!
How does Thematic pricing work?
All contracts are based on comment volume, number of datasets, how the dataset is analyzed and the support you need.
What are we like to work with?
We pride ourselves on our outstanding support! Our Customer Success team, lead by a former customer, are often described as 'legendary', 'outstanding' and 'exceptional'. Just check out our G2 reviews.
Do integrations cost extra?
There's no additional cost for our one click integrations or csv uploads. You can also use our API to push data into Thematic. We make it easy to pipe in your data.
What data can I add?
Any dataset with comments, along with the quantitative variables or metadata you'd like to include. Your comments could be survey responses, conversations, support chat, app reviews, social media data or community chat. Thematic works best with at least 2,000 rows of verbatims.
Can I do a custom demo on my own data?
Yes, we offer custom demos on your data or a comprehensive paid pilot for the complete experience of the Thematic platform.
What data protection do you have in place?
You can be sure your data and reporting is handled properly. Thematic is Soc 2 Type II and GDPR compliant with comprehensive security features and workflows.
